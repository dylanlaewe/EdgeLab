not combine the two member instances, infer correspondence between request and
projection documents, or claim sibling, grammar, reachability, event,
transaction, uniqueness, idempotency, chain, task, runtime, full-schema,
deployed-validator, public, acceptance, STOP, release, execution, wagering,
or capital meaning. It is a closed evidence grouping, not an authority grant.
