# M0.3A v13 — narrow evidence-adapter adoption

V13 supersedes v12 only as the planning adoption point for
`docs/M0.2_DECISION_EVIDENCE_ENVELOPE.v12.md`,
`schemas/m0-2-decision-evidence-envelope.v12.schema.json`,
`schemas/m0-2-engine-distribution.v5.schema.json`, and verification-result v3.

It adopts their retained, offline evidence graph for a later Data implementation.
It makes no scheduler state-machine, B0 actor, fencing, claim, transaction, event,
worker, execution, network, capital or STOP semantic change. The final R-gated table
is unchanged: only authorized evidence recording/reporting may occur, and
REPORT_BLOCKED/RECORD_EVIDENCE/REPORT_FAILURE/SAFE_YIELD never execute a workload.
Actors remain synthetic and unauthenticated.
