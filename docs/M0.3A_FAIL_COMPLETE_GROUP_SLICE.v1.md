# M0.3A FAIL and COMPLETE transaction slice v1

## Status

This document records the fifth private ordinary transaction-group increment
of the runtime-format-2 reducer. It extends the compiled worker boundary with
the claim-closing `FAIL` and `COMPLETE` operations. It is not a whole-image
verifier release.

The slice is pure, deny-only, and disconnected from the public verifier. A
candidate that passes every compiled check remains opaque as
`UNSUPPORTED / CLOSURE_NOT_COMPILED / DENY`.

## Compiled transitions

`FAIL` is valid from either `CLAIMED` or `IN_PROGRESS`. Its request carries the
exact active claim ID and fence, a closed failure code, and a failure-evidence
digest. It closes the claim as `FAILED`, moves the task to the terminal state
for that attempt, increments scheduler and dispatch generations, advances the
fence, and clears the active claim.

`COMPLETE` is valid only from `IN_PROGRESS`. Its request carries the exact
active claim ID and fence, an exact Boolean `close`, and a completion-evidence
digest. Both outcomes close the claim as `COMPLETED`, increment scheduler and
dispatch generations, advance the fence, and clear the active claim:

- `close=false` enters `EXECUTION_COMPLETED / REVIEW_PENDING`;
- `close=true` enters the closed, no-review terminal state.

Both operations are compiled after `START`, after one `CONTINUE`, and, for
`FAIL` only, directly after `CLAIM`.

Evidence codes and digests are syntactically bound replay inputs; this slice
does not establish that referenced evidence exists, is true, or is current.

## Replay boundary

The shared worker reducer independently derives and validates:

- exact global and task-local predecessor chains;
- transaction, operation-key, event-ID, and event-digest uniqueness;
- runtime, repository, request, response, and commit cross-bindings;
- prefix-derived before-state and exact claim/fence compare-and-swap;
- closed operation arguments and empty semantic payloads;
- exact after-state and immutable historical claim closure;
- attempt head, idempotency row, task row, metadata head, typed reachability,
  and all 15 table projections.

Supplied state, claim history, and tables remain comparison-only. Hostile claim
closure, after-state, fence, claim ID, close-type, and source-state mutations
fail closed. No late validation failure can publish a partial patch.

## Explicit exclusions

This version does not compile repeated continuations, `RECOVER`, `RETRY`, later
claim cycles, governance, review, route, remediation, repository import,
checkpoint, or export groups. It adds no writer, database, selector cutover,
currentness claim, positive consistency result, authority, execution, or
capital path.

## Next boundary

Compile `RECOVER` and `RETRY`, then generalize replay for repeated continuation
and repeated claim/attempt cycles without losing any historical claim or
attempt projection.
