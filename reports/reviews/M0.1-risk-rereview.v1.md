## Control-family results

### Scientific evidence and authority

`consume_scientific_commitment` correctly verifies Quant content, exact policy source/generation, authority ID/generation, and local replay state, and its result grants no lifecycle, paper, live, capital, or execution authority. Duplicate ID/digest consumption rejects across a new store instance and under concurrent local consumers.

The protection is lost downstream because `replay_lifecycle` accepts any mapping containing `scientific_integrity_consistent=true`; it does not require or bind the consumption receipt. Scientific evidence can therefore be invented or borrowed from another subject. The policy interface also depends on an external authenticator: the literal `VERIFIED_EXTERNAL` field and expected-source argument do not authenticate a policy locally. That limitation is documented. The revoked-authority and optional-authorship acceptances are separate local defects because they occur after accepting the supplied policy boundary.

Alias collision, unknown alias, source mismatch, wrong role/scope, stale policy generation, same canonical actor through two aliases, duplicate attestation identity, subject mismatch, and direct self-review reject. Pairwise conflicts among approvers do not.

### Replay and history currency

The JSON replay store provides its stated trusted-host guarantee: advisory locking serializes local consumers, ID and digest uniqueness persist across reloads, atomic replacement and fsync occur before success, and missing/corrupt state denies. A host able to restore the pre-consumption file can replay; this is expected under the documented hostile-host boundary.

No equivalent authoritative head exists for lifecycle, ledger, or kill history. Their internal hash chains detect mutation, deletion within the submitted list, reordering, and forks, but cannot detect omission of the latest valid tail. SR02 therefore remains a local current-state gap.

### Lifecycle and independent approvals

Illegal skips, direct M0 live states, future events, reordered chains, mixed strategy versions, terminal resurrection within a submitted chain, missing approvals, changed subjects, wrong roles, duplicate canonical actors, and self-review reject. A complete positive synthetic path reaches PAPER_TRADING eligibility and no execution authorization.

The positive can also be reached with an unconsumed one-bit science mapping and an unbound one-field STOP ALLOW mapping. A valid terminal tail can be omitted. Mutually conflicted Skeptic and Risk approvers are accepted. Current state is thus event-derived only within the caller-selected prefix, not from trusted current history.

### STOP

With a correct verifier-selected snapshot, the real global OPEN STOP denies affected paper eligibility and new exposure. Missing expected refs, incomplete/untrusted/stale state, unreadable or changed STOP bytes, unknown targets, dependency cycles, and dependency-scoped descendants fail closed.

The STOP record does not bind its machine action/scope mapping; the snapshot supplies those facts. Relabeling its action returns ALLOW unless an honest external producer prevents it. Candidate clearance also trusts self-declared required roles, capital implication, remediation hashes, acknowledgement, and descendant revalidation, and does not reject a future `occurred_at`. SR04 prevents reliance on clearance eligibility.

### Accounting, settlement, and reconciliation

Integer conservation, nonnegative cash/reserved/liability, exact configured opening, paper/live separation, one open reservation, complete settlement amount, duplicate release/settlement/correction rejection, event chaining, and fresh exact snapshot comparison work within the submitted event list. Arbitrary credit/debit event types reject. Settlement after release and release after settlement reject. Partial settlement is unsupported and rejects.

The reducer cannot establish that the supplied list reaches the current authoritative head. A truncated prefix plus a newly sealed fresh snapshot reconciles. Reservation/settlement records also lack an exact action/position binding. External settlement evidence authenticity remains outside local proof.

### Exposure and correlation

Position, daily, strategy, source, event, and maximum shared-key totals work for OPEN positions with exact identifiers. Required event/strategy/source/market/selection keys cannot be omitted. Mixed environments, nonpositive exposure, incomplete limits, and unresolved live limits reject.

PENDING positions are excluded, permitting multiple outstanding approvals to pass separately. All canonical-looking keys are derived from submitter-controlled labels, so synchronized cosmetic renaming avoids the represented limits. More subtle statistical dependency remains a future domain-model problem, but pending inclusion and canonical Data identity binding are required now for the represented dimensions.

### Kill switch, paper/live, capital, and per-action authorization

Given complete current history, the switch begins ENGAGED, M0 reset rejects, reset needs a current Risk attestation/remediation/health/STOP ALLOW, triggers relatch, generation changes invalidate older tokens, and exposure-neutral settlement remains allowed. A valid old history prefix defeats this because there is no authoritative current generation.

The actual repository inputs correctly deny live action: M0, LOCKED, zero deployable capital, OPEN STOP, and ENGAGED kill each remain controlling. However, `risk_decision` does not load or authenticate any of them. Passing `phase=M1`, `live_status=UNLOCKED`, positive capital, and one-field positive mappings returns a schema-valid LIVE `AUTHORIZED` decision. The same occurs for PAPER in M0. M0 safety therefore depends entirely on the caller never substituting inputs, which is not an authorization control.

The output schema clearly distinguishes science, lifecycle, paper/live eligibility, capital, controls, individual authorization, execution authorization, verdict, and reasons. The values are unsafe because their inputs are unbound. Partial-object consumers could also mistake `paper_eligible` or `live_eligible` for authorization unless they enforce the final verdict; typed exact subject binding is needed at both producer and consumer boundaries.

## Required remediation

H-M0-021 v1 assigns remediation to Risk. At minimum:

1. Define an exact typed action request binding environment, strategy/version, event, market, selection, amount, reservation, and action ID.
2. Replace bare booleans/dictionaries with schema-valid, digest-bound control results for that exact request. Bind the final decision to their digests and to verifier-controlled project phase, bankroll, policy, STOP, lifecycle, ledger, exposure, and kill generations.
3. Issue a single-use, expiring action authorization bound to the action digest, decision digest, controls digest, kill generation, and current-state generations; atomically consume it.
4. Require verifier-controlled current heads/inventories for lifecycle, ledger, STOP, exposure, and kill state. Valid prefixes must not establish currency.
5. Make scientific authorship provenance mandatory, exclude revoked actors during consumption, and enforce pairwise conflicts among every author/approver/clearer.
6. Derive STOP action/scope and clearance authorities from trusted policy and the preserved STOP, validate resolution time, and resolve evidence references rather than accepting opaque hashes/booleans.
7. Bind reservations and settlements to the exact action/position and authoritative ledger head.
8. Include pending exposures and use Data-owned canonical event/outcome identities; treat unresolved identity or correlation as denial.
