## Contracts, enforcement and finding coverage

See SPECIFICATIONS Research v1 for the complete API and migration contract.
EXPLORATORY exposure records bind purpose, actor, first access, coverage, exact
membership, hashed evidence, ancestors and correction predecessor. UNKNOWN is
recordable and blocks freshness. Exposure history traverses both ancestry and
previous versions, so a correction cannot hide a disclosed examined population.

Frozen registrations bind complete hypothesis bytes, structured justified decimal
thresholds, analysis plan, holdout custody and exploratory ancestry. Registration
ordering, custody coverage, known status and no disclosed examined overlap are
mandatory. A caller-held prior registration digest detects edited thresholds,
hypotheses or plans, including attempts to rehash child references. The timestamp
checks detect contradictory/retroactive declarations; they do not independently
prove when a human actually saw results.

Holdout access requires the exact Data membership reference (path/kind/ID/version/
byte SHA), not just equal cardinality or sample digest. Contamination comparison
uses Data's canonical namespace/event/outcome keys; same, renamed/reordered and
partially intersecting populations are rejected. First confirmatory results access
must strictly follow freeze. The positive result is explicitly only
CONSISTENT_SUPPLIED_EVIDENCE, with operational_authorization=false and caller-owned
history completeness. Contracts are synthetic-only.

## Compatibility, deferrals and limits

Legacy schemas remain byte-identical and readable, with no implicit approval or
conversion. New registries are additive. `validate_record` performs shape/clock
checks; `validate_research` performs Research semantics; `validate_repository`
invokes those semantics for persisted new records and Data's graph checks. No new
Research contract is yet compulsory for a legacy experiment. This known gap is
Quant's assigned integration, not a claim that H05 is fully solved.

Quant must enumerate all trial/exposure history (including failures), require the
contract for confirmatory execution, bind actual inputs/splits to the population,
carry exploratory/hypothesis lineage across versions, check new disclosures since
freeze, and implement reproduction bundles. The supplied check conservatively
denies any additional examined overlap; it is a first-access contract, not a
repeated evaluation/reuse policy. Quant must not merely copy candidate SHA fields
as verifier pins. H-M0-004 v6 delivers these requirements to Director for the eventual
H-M0-005 release; it does not start Quant.

Data owns real entity resolution, lineage and accepted-history primitives. Risk
owns authority, STOP/lifecycle, operational eligibility and capital. Existing
verifier-controlled checkpoint assumptions are reused, not strengthened: no external
trusted baseline, authenticated observer, sealed holdout or separate review channel
is provisioned. A malicious/compromised caller can supply a forged pin or omit
exposures; truthful access evidence and complete off-system history cannot be proven
by these local files. Hashes establish byte integrity, not external truth. Canonical
identities cannot detect dishonest upstream renaming of the underlying events.

Research does not independently approve its own protocol. Director has a reviewable
completion package, but **should not release Quant until the new probe compatibility
disposition is independently resolved** and the completion gate is verified. Interim
consultation is requested, not automatically scheduled. Full integrated Skeptic
review and all existing STOP-clearance requirements remain outstanding.
