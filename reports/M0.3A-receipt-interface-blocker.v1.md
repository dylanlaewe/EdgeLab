# M0.3A frozen M0.2 receipt/interface blocker

## Finding

The implementation-authoritative M0.3A v3 contract requires every R-gated
transition to bind the current **exact M0.2 receipt/input bundle**. Frozen M0.2
does not provide such an interface. Its only outward artifact is a deterministic
`decision_id`; [M0.2 orchestration documentation](../docs/M0.2_ORCHESTRATION.md)
explicitly says that decision IDs bind local content but are **not authenticated
receipts**. No M0.2 receipt schema, versioned immutable receipt file, canonical
input-bundle record, or verification API is published.

## Why Data cannot select a substitute

`receipt_digest` in the current M0.3A test fixture is caller supplied. Treating
it as an exact M0.2 receipt would violate v3's request binding requirements and
invent frozen Stage 1 semantics. Deriving a new receipt from private
`orchestration.decision` implementation details would similarly change its
interface and require authority outside Data's released scope. M0.3A cannot
truthfully demonstrate stale-M0.2-receipt denial, revalidation, replay binding,
or final Git binding until this contract is supplied.

## Evidence

- `docs/M0.3A_TRANSACTIONAL_CONTRACT.v3.md` §§1–4, 7, 9: exact R receipt/input
  bundle is required for transitions, canonical request content, events and
  replay.
- `docs/M0.2_ORCHESTRATION.md`: decision IDs are explicitly not receipts.
- `src/edgelab/orchestration.py`: `decision` returns a decision ID, dispatch
  status and mock-only metadata; it persists no receipt or input-bundle object.
- Freeze preservation check: `git diff --exit-code
  bc3a40924ca580ca6eddc4c24e32ff99ddd95330 -- src/edgelab/orchestration.py
  tests/test_orchestration.py tests/test_orchestration_final.py` passed.

## Required resolution

Director plus the M0.2 contract owner must publish a reviewed, immutable adapter
contract that defines the exact bytes/digests, typed repository locations,
version/currentness behavior and verification procedure for an M0.2 receipt and
its inputs. Alternatively, they may revise M0.3A v3 through its documented
acceptance process. Data must not clear this STOP, change M0.2, or replace the
required receipt with a caller assertion.

`STOP-M0-001` remains OPEN. This is an additional scoped STOP, not a clearance
or a change to M0/M1, executor, research or capital boundaries.
