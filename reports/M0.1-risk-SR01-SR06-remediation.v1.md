## Root cause and authoritative assembly

SR01 arose because the old final function accepted caller-provided summaries of
science, lifecycle, phase, capital, STOP, ledger, exposure, kill and individual
authorization. A caller could compose a plausible object and obtain AUTHORIZED.
The compatibility entry point is now permanently deny-only. The new boundary takes
one typed exact action and a verifier-controlled `RiskStateAuthority`; Risk obtains
an action-bound expiring current-state generation, validates every underlying
record/reducer itself, re-reads the generation before commit, and emits a token only
after an AUTHORIZED decision. Fixed path-and-byte bindings resolve project state,
bankroll and Risk policy. Ledger opening capital and exposure limits are derived
from those records, not accepted from the bundle.

Scientific input is the full Quant assessment and structured commitment. It must
be durably consumed once, identify the exact strategy/version/experiment, retain
all false operational claims, and include complete canonical authorship provenance.
Revoked, unknown, omitted, self-issued, stale-generation or conflicted authority
denies. Lifecycle consumes the receipt; a Boolean consistency flag is insufficient.

The per-action authorization is single-use and bounded by action/state expiry. It
binds action ID/digest/type, strategy/version and canonical identities, environment,
event/market/selection, amount, price constraint, position, reservation/related
action, decision/control digests, authority/current-state and kill generations,
exposure inventory and ledger head. Action, amount, paper/live, price, identity,
position, generation and expiry mutations all reject.

- SR03: canonical actor provenance, active/revoked status, authority generation,
  self-review and pairwise conflict checks apply to scientific evidence, lifecycle,
  STOP clearance and reset attestations. Real-world identity authentication remains
  an external assumption.
- SR04: STOP applicability derives from typed action identity, exact canonical
  dependencies, immutable repository STOP inventory and verifier stop policy.
  Snapshot labels and candidate clearance roles are ignored. Missing dependencies,
  deleted/incomplete inventory, wrong heads, changed bytes and future/stale/wrong
  clearance deny. The actual STOP remains OPEN.
- SR05: PENDING and OPEN positions consume exposure. Strategy, source, event,
  market and selection reduce through verified canonical namespace/ID, so cosmetic
  aliases and identity-record versions cannot create diversification. Unknown
  identity or dependencies deny; no complete domain correlation model is claimed.
- SR06: reservation, release, settlement and correction bind action, position,
  strategy/version, account, canonical event/market/selection, amount,
  authorization and economic identity. Cross-environment, changed amount/position/
  event/action, released substitution, unrelated reservation and changed-wrapper
  duplicate settlement attacks reject.

## Positive controls and interface

synthetic M1 fixture can reach paper lifecycle eligibility and exact new-exposure
authorization only when every current control passes. An exact pre-existing
exposure-neutral settlement remains possible under an engaged kill switch; it
cannot create or replace exposure. Against the actual repository, a live action is
DENIED and forged phase, bankroll path or opening capital rejects.

Decision fields remain separate: science accepted, lifecycle eligible, paper
eligible, live eligible, capital available, controls current, individual action
authorized and final verdict. No partial result or eligibility object is an
execution authorization.
