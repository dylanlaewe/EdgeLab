## Q01 root cause and remediation

The previous `_deny_prior_holdout_use` implementation inspected only earlier
`test` and `holdout` roles and skipped every trial without a results-access
reference. A population previously consumed through train or validation could
therefore be proposed as a fresh confirmatory holdout. The skip also treated
absence of results-access evidence as proof that failed, cancelled or incomplete
work had not consumed its declared inputs.

The successor scans the complete current accepted trial inventory already required
by the Quant current-state contract. For every trial other than the claimed trial
whose `started_at` is no later than the proposed holdout's first access, it resolves
every declared train, validation, test and holdout manifest to canonical Data
membership keys. Any intersection with the proposed holdout rejects freshness.
The rule is independent of filename, manifest ID, membership ID/version, ordering,
packaging and split label. Exact, renamed, reordered, partial, versioned and
split-role-relabeled overlap therefore reject.

Missing access evidence is not evidence of non-consumption. Earlier FAILED,
CANCELLED and ATTEMPTED trials with overlapping declared inputs fail closed. The
rule remains scoped to the proposed confirmatory holdout: prior reuse of unrelated
train populations is allowed, and work beginning after the proposed first holdout
access does not retroactively change whether that holdout was fresh at access.

Owner successors reject exact prior train, validation, test and holdout use;
renamed/reordered/partial/versioned train use; partial validation use; role
relabeling; and failed/cancelled/incomplete overlap. The disjoint confirmation and
non-holdout-reuse controls remain positive with all operational authorization
fields false.

## Q02 root cause and remediation

The previous authority adapter could return any nonempty string. Quant checked
neither token structure nor its relationship to the assessment, accepted state,
authority generation or experiment.

The successor returns a schema-valid `authority-commitment` record. Its canonical
self-digest and local verifier bind:

- authority ID and monotonic generation;
- unique commitment ID and commitment timestamp;
- exact assessment digest;
- exact current-state digest;
- experiment identity digest;
- a digest of the exact protocol, preregistration, trial, inventory, reproduction
  and results-access references; and
- explicit limitations.

`verify_authority_commitment` independently recomputes the assessment,
claim-reference and commitment digests and checks every binding. Owner attacks
reject opaque tokens, missing/extra fields, another assessment, another state,
another authority, stale generation, mismatched experiment identity and malformed
commitments.

This is local content binding only. It does not authenticate the external
authority, prove durable storage, prove that compare-and-commit was truly atomic,
establish key custody or make a malicious/colluding adapter honest. Risk still owns
authority authentication and trust policy. No production authority or signature
trust root is provisioned.
